## Question

Which real projects, apps, repositories, screenshots, technical capabilities, founder credentials, or other evidence may appear publicly, and what verifiable source supports each item?

## Resolution

The enrollment-first website may reference these five real products, all explicitly presented as works in development:

- **Walk Blocker**
- **Macro Chef**
- **Colofon** — an app for clearing a reading backlog
- **Music discovery tracker** — working descriptive label for an unnamed app that tracks albums and influencer recommendations
- **Game discovery app** — working descriptive label for an unnamed app that helps gamers choose what to play next

Do not publish a percentage-complete figure, imply that any app is available, or add unsupported users, clients, testimonials, metrics, store links, or launch dates. Screenshots, prototypes, repositories, and detailed case studies are not required for the enrollment-first release and can be added later.
